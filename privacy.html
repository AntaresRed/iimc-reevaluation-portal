Privacy Policy

Re-Evaluation Portal · Last updated 25 September 2026

The Re-Evaluation Portal lets students ask for a re-evaluation of their exam answers and lets faculty review those requests. This page explains what information the portal collects, who can see it, and how long it is kept.

Who can use the portal

Only people who sign in with an @email.iimcal.ac.in Google account can use the portal. Sign-ins from any other address are refused.

What we collect

  • From your Google account: your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, contacts or personal Drive files.
  • What you enter in a request: your name, registration number, section, the questions you want re-evaluated and your reasons.
  • Photos you upload of your answer script.
  • The outcome: the professor's original and updated marks, their remarks, and a history of changes to the request's status.

How it is used

Your information is used only to process your re-evaluation request and keep a record of the decision. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside the people listed below.

Who can see your request

  • You.
  • The professor or professors who teach your section of that course.
  • Portal administrators in the MBA office, who can view every request but cannot change a professor's decision.

Where it is stored

  • Requests and decisions are stored in a database hosted by Supabase.
  • Photos are stored in a Google Drive account used only by the portal. The portal can reach only the files it created itself.
  • The website runs on Vercel.

How long it is kept

  • Photos of your answer script are deleted 30 days after the professor records a final decision.
  • The request itself, the marks and the remarks are kept as the record of the re-evaluation.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information held about you, or ask for a correction, by writing to the contact below.
